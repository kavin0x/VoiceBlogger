# Privacy Policy for VoiceBlogger

**Last updated: October 9th, 2026**

## Overview

VoiceBlogger is designed with privacy as a core principle. All AI processing — speech recognition and blog generation — happens entirely on your device. Voice Blogger has no account of its own. Optional iCloud sync, if you turn it on, stores a private copy in your iCloud.

## Data We Collect

**We collect no personal data.** VoiceBlogger has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics.

## What Stays on Your Device

The following data is created on your device:

- **Voice recordings** — audio files captured through your microphone
- **Transcriptions** — text generated from your recordings by the on-device Whisper model
- **Blog posts** — content generated by the on-device Gemma language model
- **App preferences** — settings stored in iOS UserDefaults (e.g., whether models have been downloaded)

None of this data is transmitted to us. If you leave iCloud sync off, it stays in the app sandbox on that device.

## Optional iCloud sync

During setup, and later in Settings, you can choose to sync posts, notes, captions, your personal dictionary, and recordings through your private iCloud to your iPhone, iPad, and Mac. That copy is in your iCloud account, not on a Voice Blogger server. Speech recognition, writing, and the downloaded AI models stay on each device and are not uploaded.

Turning sync off on a device keeps a local copy there and stops new uploads from that device. Data already in iCloud remains until you delete Voice Blogger’s iCloud data from the system iCloud settings.

## AI Models

On first launch, VoiceBlogger downloads two AI model files from [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co)

Once downloaded, the models are stored on your device and all inference runs locally. Hugging Face's own [privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy) governs those download requests. If you turn on iCloud sync, the app also talks to iCloud to sync your library.

## Permissions

| Permission     | Why It's Needed                        |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Microphone** | To record your voice for transcription |

No other device permissions are requested or used.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party — because we never receive it in the first place. If you turn on iCloud sync, Apple stores that library in your private iCloud under Apple’s privacy policy.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Recordings and blog posts are stored in the app sandbox, and in your iCloud only when you turn sync on. You can delete them at any time from within the app. Uninstalling VoiceBlogger removes the local copy. iCloud data is removed when you delete Voice Blogger’s iCloud data in system settings.

## Children's Privacy

VoiceBlogger does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, no special handling is required.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and note the change in the app's release notes.
